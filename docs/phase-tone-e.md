# Tone upgrade E — Feedback and interaction

Status: complete (2026-07-22).

J and O toggle shadow and highlight threshold views; both may be enabled, and
holding Alt during a pointer drag temporarily enables both. Thresholds are HDR
aware. The histogram is draggable in five regions mapped to Blacks, Shadows,
Exposure, Highlights, and Whites. Auto exposure is available as a button and
via Shift-double-click on Exposure.

The debug view is a uniform branch inside every interactive output transform;
normal rendering stays on the previous path.
