Reference white protected

The new HDR shoulder begins at or near reference white and spends the selected headroom above it. Highlights retain pop before rolling gently toward the display limit.

±1.75 EV

Auto guardrail

Full Auto Balance cannot flatten a deliberately low-key or high-key photograph into the same mid-grey result.

11

Retuned looks

Built-in recipes now use smaller, coordinated moves. Applying a look preserves scene brightness and endpoints unless intentionally overridden.

Design decision: camera-faithful color and editable latitude come first. “Pop” comes from controlled separation around diffuse white—not clipping or aggressive local contrast.

Evidence standard

The research favors first-party documentation and published technical work: Apple EDR and Adaptive HDR, Android Ultra HDR, ACES 2, Adobe Camera Raw/Lightroom, Capture One, Google HDR+, and Hasselblad Phocus/HNCS. Model reviews were advisory; every adopted change was inspected and tested locally.
