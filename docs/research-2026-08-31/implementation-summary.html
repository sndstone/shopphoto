Knowledge group 06

What shipped, and why.

This page maps the research to concrete changes and validation. It is the shortest route through the upgrade for a maintainer.

OverviewRestart notebook

Imaging engine

  • CPU and GPU HDR shoulder use the same near-white, monotonic tanh roll-off.
  • The EDR path measures and shapes Rec.2020 luminance before converting primaries, matching PQ export; a final hue-preserving peak guard handles gamut-transform overshoot.
  • SDR fallback compresses luminance and scales RGB uniformly, preventing avoidable hue drift. SDR edits on a float EDR surface retain the same component-clamped appearance as the ordinary output path.
  • Auto Balance reads robust luminance percentiles, blends p90/p99.5 highlight pressure, and uses conservative parameter caps.
  • Deep backlit frames receive a bounded encoded-domain mid-shadow lift that pins black and reference white.
  • Full Auto samples a hidden neutral-tone render; Exposure Only uses a fresh current-edit histogram. The dedicated analysis readback cannot be consumed by a viewport refresh; edits made mid-analysis trigger fresh analysis, and failed GPU readbacks retry twice before exiting visibly.
  • Auto Defringe now records purple/green confidence and matching full-resolution width planes at five scales. Narrow CA keeps its conservative brighter-neighbor test; wide bloom uses half/full-radius neutral references, sampled hue ranges, and perceived-luminance compensation.
  • The proxy defringe pass was moved to its intended post-tone position, full-resolution tile radius no longer shrinks with tile width, and tile apron sizing now covers the larger of local-contrast and fringe requirements.
  • The XCD selector is preloaded for missing maker-note identification. This remains separate from correction profiles, so ShopPhoto never labels an opaque Phocus GMap or identity grid as applied calibration.
  • Shader and unit tests cover reference white, monotonicity, bounded peak, headroom usage, negative matrix values, HDR-tail recovery, convergence, backlight, low/high-key separation, narrow purple/green fringe detection, flat-color protection, broad eclipse-like bloom, radius estimation, and luma-safe correction.

Presets and camera workflow

  • All built-in recipes were reduced to end-use-ready strengths and checked across six real Hasselblad files.
  • A final endpoint audit over 78 real-file recipe renders caught the Nature preset’s dehaze-induced shadow clipping: on the extreme backlit frame, near-black pixels fell from 15.00% to 0.02% after removing preset dehaze and reducing overlapping texture/clarity.
  • Creative look curves now remain near the finished Standard rendering instead of causing an unrelated midtone collapse.
  • Preset application preserves scene exposure, bounded brightness lift, white/black endpoints unless the recipe deliberately defines them, plus lens and other image-specific state.
  • Camera profile saturation was softened, especially the former Flat profile.
  • “Flat Proof” is now “Soft Neutral”.
  • Private-equivalence wording and calibration constants were removed; only public facts, user measurements, and ShopPhoto-authored processing remain.

The macOS image stages the universal app, an Applications shortcut, this complete self-contained research notebook, and a one-click notebook opener together.

Recommended workflow: open RAW → choose camera/base profile and restrained look → use Auto Balance if needed (or re-run it after changing the look) → adjust highlight recovery/headroom while viewing on the intended display → export.

Known scope boundaries

Single-frame automatic tone cannot create the motion robustness or noise performance of a phone’s multi-frame HDR capture. Display-dependent HDR appearance also requires a compatible panel, OS surface, and metadata-aware viewer. The upgrade makes those limits explicit while improving the behavior inside them.
