Knowledge group 04

Learn from Phocus. Don’t imitate its internals.

Phocus provides the most relevant product reference for Hasselblad files. Its observable workflow can guide ShopPhoto; its camera calibration, LUTs, film curves, and proprietary algorithms cannot be copied.

OverviewReference Match guideNext: UI design

Observed capabilities

Highlight workflow

For X2D II 100C files, the Phocus 4.0.1 read-me describes Dynamic Range Compression with content-selected “As Shot” highlight/shadow levels. Its broader HDR workflow has a separate Recovery setting, a cross-mode ghost marker, a Limit control, and a histogram marker for monitor highlight capability.

Mobile HDR

The current Phocus Mobile page documents HDR display/editing, Ultra HDR JPEG and HDR TIFF output. It also publishes explicit OS, memory, and chipset constraints.
