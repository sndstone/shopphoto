From one-pixel color fringe to eclipse-scale bloom.

The eclipse example is not ordinary lateral chromatic aberration. It combines a broad magenta/green axial halo with clipped highlight regions. ShopPhoto now treats those as two correction scales, supports an on-image hue picker, and keeps the repair luminance-safe.

What the eclipse screenshot shows

The colored bands are tens of displayed pixels wide, whereas profile-based transverse CA is usually corrected by shifting channels a few pixels. Pure white patches contain no recoverable RGB detail; a defringe tool can neutralize the colored transition around them, but it cannot reconstruct solar-corona texture after all channels clip.

Why the old controls stalled: the previous neighborhood ended at 5 px and full-resolution tiles reduced that radius a second time. Its reference search also assumed a brighter neutral neighbor, which misses parts of a broad halo lying between black and white.

Two related but distinct repair modes

ModeEvidence requiredReference and safety
Narrow CATarget hue excess, a local saturation outlier, a strong dark-to-highlight transition and a brighter low-saturation neighbor.Eight directions at the selected radius. Conservative ceiling protects legitimate subject color.
Wide / clipped bloomSampled target hue, a local saturation outlier, a bright edge and a large luminance range across the neighborhood.Sixteen samples at half and full radius can borrow neutral chromaticity from either side. Perceptual luminance compensation prevents a desaturated band from looking brighter.

The correction runs after camera/profile color and tone, but before clarity and sharpening. It transfers local neutral chromaticity and preserves the center brightness; it does not blur geometry or paint across non-target hues.

Recommended eclipse workflow

  1. Press Auto Defringe. Auto now estimates purple amount, green amount and full-resolution fringe width, and enables wide mode when the evidence is broad.
  2. If a residual remains, press Pick purple… and click directly on the magenta halo. Repeat with Pick green… on the inner green rim. Each pick sets a narrow ±18° starting range that can be widened manually.
  3. Enable Wide / clipped highlight bloom when the colored band is visibly wider than ordinary edge CA. Increase Fringe width until the whole band is covered, then stop.
  4. Raise Edge protection if legitimate purple or green subject edges are affected. Judge at 1:1 and compare with the master checkbox.

Hasselblad documents the same useful interaction principle in Phocus: pick an affected area to obtain automatic amount and range, then adjust manually. ShopPhoto’s implementation is independent and adds multi-scale width estimation for broad bloom.

Scope

This stage repairs longitudinal/axial purple and green fringing and broad chromatic highlight bloom. Profile-based transverse CA—radial channel displacement—is a different optical problem. Lens identity and calibration status are documented separately; a lens name alone is not correction data.
