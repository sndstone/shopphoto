01

Before/after for every edit: toggle, draggable split, side by side

HighEffort M

The only general comparison is \, which swaps the whole frame to the original. The only sign that it is active is an 11 px amber BEFORE (\) in the top bar, far from the image. There is a well-built draggable split with BEFORE/AFTER labels, but it only works for the AI denoise comparison, and you reach it through a checkbox buried in the Detail card.

panels/chrome.rs:275–282 · panels/viewport.rs:465–511 · panels/develop.rs:946–967

  • Generalise the existing split: "before" means the default EditState, or any snapshot or history step (see 12).
  • Modes are Toggle, Split and Side by side. \ toggles and Y cycles modes, as in Lightroom.
  • Labels sit on the image, and the divider has a wide grab area.
  • Keep the denoise split as a special case: its before is the untouched sensor data.
Before current behaviour

Try: press \ (or Press \), then find the indicator: it is in the top bar, not on the image.

After proposal

Try: drag the divider, switch modes, press Y to cycle and \ to toggle.

02

Floating viewport toolbar: zoom presets, clipping toggles, navigator

HighEffort M

Zoom has two states: a Fit pill in the top bar toggles to 100%, and pinch zooms freely. When you are zoomed into a 100 MP X2D file, nothing shows where you are in the frame. Clipping overlays are J and O, and their only visible switches are two checkboxes at the bottom of the View menu. The zoom level is repeated as faint 11 px text in the viewport corner.

panels/chrome.rs:166–172, 256–266 · panels/viewport.rs:513–525

  • A compact toolbar at the bottom of the viewport: Fit · Fill · 50 · 100 · 200, the two clipping toggles lit when on, and before/after.
  • A navigator appears while zoomed. Drag its rectangle to pan, or click anywhere in it to jump.
  • This frees top-bar space for 16.
Before current behaviour

Try: click Fit to zoom, drag to pan, and press O or J for the clipping overlays.

After proposal

Try: pick a zoom level, drag the navigator rectangle, and toggle the clipping buttons.

03

Readable tool-mode banner with Done and Cancel

MediumEffort S

Six tool modes (colour-range pick, curve pipette, defringe pick, guided upright, heal/clone/brush, AI point pick) each paint their instruction straight onto the photo. It is white 11 px text with no backdrop, so on a bright sky it disappears. Crop tells you to go back to the Geometry card to finish, and none of the modes has a clickable way out.

panels/viewport.rs:357–363, 384–390, 440–446, 577–586, 755–761, 843–849, 1231–1237

  • One shared tool_banner(ui, icon, title, hint) helper: a translucent pill, centred at the top of the viewport.
  • Clickable Cancel and Done buttons with Esc and ↵ hints, the same in every mode.
  • It stays readable on any image, and all seven call sites, crop included, switch to the one helper.
Before current behaviour

Try: pick a tool below the image and look for the instruction in the sky.

After proposal

Try: pick a tool, click the image, then use Done, Cancel, ↵ or Esc.

04

Crop overlay: edge handles, thirds grid, aspect lock, Enter to commit

HighEffort M

The crop rectangle has four small 3.6 px accent dots as handles, with no edge handles and no grid. Aspect ratios are one-shot menu items in the Geometry card, so the first corner drag breaks the ratio. There is no readout of the output size. You finish by toggling Crop in the inspector again.

panels/viewport.rs:1165–1237 · panels/develop.rs:1270–1301 · panels/chrome.rs:5–14

  • L-shaped corner handles and edge handles with hit targets of at least 14 px.
  • A rule-of-thirds grid while dragging. A locked aspect ratio that holds during corner drags, and X swaps orientation.
  • A live pixel and megapixel readout, useful before 2×2 supersampling.
  • ↵ commits, Esc reverts, and the banner from 3 carries the same actions.
Before current behaviour

Try: grab a corner dot, which needs to be within 14 px, or drag inside to move. Apply an aspect ratio and then drag a corner.

After proposal

Try: drag corners or edges, lock an aspect ratio, press X to rotate it, and ↵ to apply.

05

Sliders: fill from zero, units, visible modified state, reset, scrub and type

HighEffort S

slider_trailing_fill = true fills every track from its left end. A neutral Tint of 0 on a −150…150 range therefore looks half-applied, and the accent colour stops meaning "changed". Every value uses fixed_decimals(1), which gives "5500.0" for kelvin and "1.3" for exposure, with no units. The only modified marker is a 1.8 px dot. Reset exists as a double-click, but nothing in the UI shows it.

theme.rs:81 · panels/develop.rs:44–122 (param_slider), 19–38 (fixed_drag_value)

  • Bipolar ranges fill from zero, with a tick at the neutral point. Unipolar ranges keep the left fill.
  • A per-parameter format: +1.30 EV, 5 500 K, −14, 1.0 px.
  • Changed values turn accent and a reset ↺ appears on hover. Double-click still works.
  • Scrub the label, click the number to type, use arrow keys on the thumb (⇧ ×10, ⌥ ×0.1).
  • Keep fixed_drag_value so the row width stays stable while dragging, which is the existing and important guarantee.
Before current behaviour

Try: which sliders are actually changed? Double-click a label to reset it.

After proposal

Try: drag a label left or right, click a number and type, hover a changed row for ↺, or Tab to a thumb and use the arrow keys.

06

Perceptual temperature scale with a colour track

MediumEffort S

Temp runs linearly from 2 000 to 50 000 K. The default of 5 500 K sits at 7% of the track, and the 2 500–10 000 K band that covers almost every photo gets about 16% of a roughly 150 px track. In that band one pixel of handle travel is over 300 K, which makes fine white-balance work a job for the number field.

panels/develop.rs:731–739

  • Map position through log(K), or mireds, so steps feel even to the eye. 5 500 K lands near a third of the track and the daylight band gets roughly 2.7× more room.
  • Paint the track blue→amber for Temp and green→magenta for Tint, so the direction is obvious without reading.
  • Add a small eyedropper beside Temp to sample a neutral point.
Before linear 2 000–50 000 K

Try: land on exactly 6 500 K by dragging, and check the K-per-pixel readout.

After log scale

Try: the same target, 6 500 K. The ruler shows where the room went.

07

Card headers show edits, reset one group, bypass a group, solo mode

HighEffort S

A collapsed card shows only its title, so you cannot tell whether Color or Effects contains edits without opening every card. There is exactly one reset: Reset all edits, placed between HDR and Metadata. You cannot reset only the Detail card, or switch a group off briefly to judge what it adds.

panels/develop.rs:124–144 (tool_group), 1472–1478

  • Header badge with the number of changed controls. Hover shows ↺ reset for that group.
  • An eye toggle bypasses the whole group for a quick before/after of just that group, with no history entry.
  • Optional solo mode (Lightroom's ⌥-click): opening one card closes the others.
  • Implementation: tool_group takes a modified: usize and returns header actions. EditState sub-structs already derive PartialEq against Default.
Before current behaviour

Try: collapse the cards. Which ones contain edits?

After proposal

Try: click an eye to bypass a group, hover a header to reset it, and turn on Solo.

08

Inspector navigation: sticky histogram, section jump bar, tool search, advanced disclosure

HighEffort M

The inspector is one long scroll of 11 cards, and the histogram scrolls away with it. Lens Corrections expands to about 25 controls: two pickers, eight defringe sliders, three calibration buttons and a status line. That buries the three things you touch on most photos. There is no quick way to reach "Dehaze" or "Defringe" by name.

panels/develop.rs:418–1522 (right_panel), 1007–1245 (Lens)

  • Histogram and a jump bar are pinned at the top: Light · Color · Detail · Optics · Geometry · Output. The bar highlights the section in view.
  • Tool search. Type "fringe" and only the matching controls stay, with their cards open.
  • Each card shows its essentials, with an Advanced · N more disclosure whose state is remembered per card.
Before current behaviour

Try: find the Green fringe slider, then scroll back to the histogram.

After proposal

Try: type fringe in search, click the jump chips, and open Advanced in Optics.

09

Histogram as a visible control: region highlight, clipping triangles, EXIF line

MediumEffort S

The histogram already works as a control. Dragging it horizontally edits Blacks, Shadows, Exposure, Highlights or Whites depending on where you start. Only a hover tooltip says so: there is no region highlight, no value readout and no cursor change. The clipping overlays have no presence here and live in the View menu.

panels/develop.rs:1571–1695 · panels/chrome.rs:256–266

  • On hover, tint the region under the pointer and label it. While dragging, show the value live.
  • Shadow and highlight clipping triangles sit in the corners. They light up when there is clipping, and a click toggles J or O.
  • A one-line EXIF strip underneath: ISO · shutter · aperture · focal length. The data is already in state.meta.
Before current behaviour

Try: drag across the histogram. Which control are you changing?

After proposal

Try: hover the regions, drag Exposure right until the triangle lights, then click the triangle.

10

Colour mixer with all bands visible, and real grading wheels

MediumEffort M

HSL is eight unlabelled 22×16 swatches and three sliders for one band at a time, so you cannot see the whole adjustment at once. Colour grading is named grading_wheel, but it is a plain 0–360 Hue slider plus Amount for each range. You pick a hue by number, with no colour on the track.

panels/develop.rs:1835–1909

  • A mixer with Hue / Saturation / Luminance tabs. Eight labelled sliders with gradient tracks show what each band does.
  • Three real wheels (shadows, midtones, highlights). Drag the puck for hue and amount, double-click to reset, and ⇧-drag keeps the hue fixed.
  • The preview strip shows the combined grade across the tonal range.
Before current behaviour

Try: give the shadows a teal cast. Which number is teal?

After proposal

Try: drag the pucks, switch mixer tabs, and double-click a wheel to reset it.

11

Presets with thumbnails, hover preview and an Amount slider

MediumEffort M

The presets rail is a column of full-width text buttons. You learn what a preset does only by applying it, which adds an undo step each time. The Look combo box already previews on hover (look_preview), but presets do not. There is no way to apply a preset at partial strength, and edits made afterwards show as a trailing *.

panels/chrome.rs:522–657 · panels/develop.rs:691–700 (look hover preview)

  • A two-column thumbnail grid. Each tile renders the current photo through the preset, reusing the thumbnail proxy.
  • Hover previews on the main image without touching history. Click commits.
  • An Amount slider (0–100%) interpolates between the pre-preset state and the preset.
  • "Modified after applying" becomes a visible chip with a one-click re-apply.
Before current behaviour

Try: compare Film Warm with Film Cool. You have to click each one.

After proposal

Try: hover the tiles to preview, click to apply, then change Amount.

12

History that names each step, shows relative time, and previews on hover

HighEffort S

Every recorded history entry is labelled "Edit". The row prints created_s raw, so the list reads Edit · 1790512345, Edit · 1790512391… and cannot be used to find a step. "Snapshots & history" is also the only plain collapsing header in the inspector that is not styled as a card.

state.rs:536–540 (label: "Edit") · panels/develop.rs:447–525

  • Label each step from a diff of the EditState before and after, for example "Exposure +0.60", "Highlights −14" or "Preset: Film Warm". Merge multi-control changes as "Basic · 3 changes".
  • Relative times ("2 min ago"), with the absolute time on hover.
  • Hover a step to preview it in the viewport. Click to jump. Steps after the current one stay greyed out as redo.
  • Snapshots get thumbnails and a "compare with current" action, which feeds 1.
Before current behaviour

Try: return to the state from just before the preset was applied.

After proposal

Try: hover the steps to preview, click to jump, and save a snapshot.

Culling and organising
13

Library grid: select without leaving the grid, rate the selection, visible flags

HighEffort M

A plain click on a grid cell opens the photo and switches to Develop immediately. Selecting therefore needs ⌘, and there is no ⇧ range select. Rating keys 0–5, P and X apply to the photo open in Develop, not to what is selected in the grid. Ratings are drawn as ☆ text glyphs, and flags as the letters P and X in accent blue, so a reject looks exactly as prominent as a pick.

panels/library.rs:702–764 · app/shortcuts.rs:106–144

  • Click selects, ⇧ selects a range, ⌘ toggles, and double-click or ↵ opens in Develop. Arrow keys move the focus.
  • Rating, flag and colour-label keys apply to every selected photo.
  • Stars become clickable on hover. Pick is a green ✓, reject a red ✕ with the thumbnail dimmed, and colour labels show as a stripe.
Before current behaviour

Try: ⌘-click three photos and press 3. Then click a photo without ⌘.

After proposal

Try: click, then ⇧-click, press 3, P, X or 6–9, click the stars, and double-click to open.

14

Filter bar with removable chips, and a selection action bar

MediumEffort M

The library header is one long row: search, Rating ≥ 0 1 2 3 4 5, flag, file type, colour, a Metadata filters menu, Smart collections and sort. Under it, the file operations (a rename-pattern field, Rename, Move…, Trash…, Undo file move) are always visible. When no photo is selected they sit greyed out. Nothing shows which filters are active or how many photos they hide.

panels/library.rs:234–545

  • A compact bar: search, clickable star threshold, flag toggles, colour dots, More ▾.
  • Every active filter becomes a chip with ×, plus "Showing 4 of 11" and Clear. The same chips can be saved as a smart collection.
  • File operations move to a bar that slides in only when there is a selection. Rename opens a popover with a live before→after name preview.
Before current behaviour

Try: set Rating ≥ 3 and flag Picked, then work out why the grid is so short.

After proposal

Try: add filters, remove chips, select photos, then open Rename.

15

Filmstrip: aspect-true cells, rating and flag badges, position counter

MediumEffort S

Filmstrip cells are fixed 112×68 grey boxes, so portrait frames float in the middle with a lot of empty grey. The current photo gets a 2 px accent stroke outside the cell, and a selected photo gets a 2 px accent stroke inside it. The two look almost the same. The filmstrip shows no rating or flag, and no position ("12 of 148"). A file without a thumbnail shows its raw file name as text (visible in the screenshot).

panels/library.rs:111–227

  • Cells take the thumbnail's aspect ratio at a fixed height.
  • Current = accent ring plus lift. Selected = soft accent wash. The two are clearly different.
  • Stars, flag and edited badges sit along the bottom edge. A position counter and ‹ › buttons sit at the left.
  • Loading cells shimmer and show a type badge instead of a file name.
Before current behaviour

Try: ⌘-click a few cells. Which one is open, and which are only selected?

After proposal

Try: use ← and →, ⌘-click, and press 1–5 or P.

How the app talks back
16

Activity indicator for background jobs, and a tidier status cluster

MediumEffort S

When a batch Auto Finish, model download or AI mask runs, the only signal is a bullet in Tools •. Progress and Cancel are two menus deep (Tools ▸ Batch). The model-download progress bar exists only inside the Detail card. The SDR/HDR badge is a Frame in a right-to-left layout, and it renders taller than the pills next to it, which is visible in the screenshot above. PROOF and BEFORE labels pop in and out of the same row and shift it.

panels/chrome.rs:62, 115–144, 272–348 · panels/develop.rs:973–979

  • A progress ring in the top bar shows the job count and overall progress. Clicking it opens a popover with each job, a progress bar, the elapsed time and Cancel.
  • When a job finishes, the ring briefly becomes a ✓ and a toast is posted (see 17).
  • View state moves to the viewport toolbar (2). The HDR/SDR badge becomes a fixed-height pill of the same size as its neighbours.
Before current behaviour

Try: start a job, then find its progress and cancel it.

After proposal

Try: start both jobs, click the ring, and cancel one.

17

Toasts with Undo, dismiss, pause on hover, and errors that stay

MediumEffort S

Every toast, including errors like "Could not export presets: …", disappears after a fixed 6 s. You cannot dismiss, pause or copy them. "Moved 3 photo(s) to trash" and "Settings synced" have undo operations in the app, but those are not offered where you would reach for them. Toasts also have no icon, so info and error differ only by text colour.

state.rs:18, 601–614 · panels/dialogs.rs:825–863

  • An icon and a countdown bar. Hover pauses the countdown, and × dismisses.
  • Errors stay until dismissed and have Copy details.
  • An optional action button, wired to the existing undo paths: file_move_undo_requested, undo_sync_requested.
  • At most three are visible, and older ones collapse into "+2 more".
Before current behaviour

Try: trigger an error and try to read it slowly.

After proposal

Try: trash photos and click Undo, hover a toast, and trigger an error.

18

Complete, searchable shortcut sheet, plus fixes for Tab and ⌘ leaks

MediumEffort S

The ? sheet lists 10 shortcuts. The handler implements more than 25, including J, O, C, M, U, 6–9, ⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z and ⇧-double-click for Auto Exposure. Two behaviours conflict. Tab always hides the side panels, even though the accessibility audit relies on Tab for focus traversal. E, D, G, P and X ignore modifiers, so ⌘E also opens Export.

panels/dialogs.rs:865–893 · app/shortcuts.rs:74–146 · docs/accessibility-audit.md

  • Generate the sheet from the same table the handler uses, grouped and searchable. Pressing a key highlights its row.
  • Tooltips append the shortcut, for example "Export the current developed image · E".
  • Only toggle panels on Tab when no widget has focus (ctx.memory(|m| m.focused().is_none())). Require modifiers.is_none() for single-letter keys.
Before current behaviour

Try: how do you show highlight clipping?

After proposal

Try: type clip, or press any key, such as O or ⌘Z.
